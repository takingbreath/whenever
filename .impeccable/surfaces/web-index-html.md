# Whenever app screens

Scope: the whole web app (web/index.html, app.js, style.css): Start, Live, My Whenevers, Whenever detail, Create (templates, When/Then builder, Check, writing progress), Activity with Reports, Actions. Visitor mode: Operate.

Audience and job: owners of homes and small shops on a phone, and CCTV operators at a desk. They turn a sentence into a working Whenever, see that it watches, trust each alert by its proof, and connect actions.

## Direction contract

THESIS: IFTTT's grammar played straight (bold colour cards, one sentence per rule, pill buttons, a five-tab shell), with Whenever's own twist: the sentence is the program. Refuses a dashboard of panels and a developer console.

OWN-WORLD: neutral #F3F3F1 ground, white surfaces, ink #121316, Schibsted Grotesk only (400 to 900). Whenever cards in six fixed hues (indigo #3346D3, jade #0E8A63, peacock #0B7285, plum #7B3FB2, marigold #F2A20A, ink #17181C) with a quiet inset keyline. Red #E5322D is reserved for "fired". Drawn 1.75-stroke icons.

STORY: the visitor sees what watches now, makes a Whenever in one sentence with slots, checks how Spark read it before money is spent, and opens any alert to its proof frame.

FIRST VIEWPORT: My Whenevers. Large title, segmented filter, a stack of colour cards: camera line and switch on top, the sentence large, a footer with runs and last time; a red tab slides out under a card that fired. Bottom tab bar on a phone, left rail on a desktop, Create in the centre.

FORM: canon (the category standard) with Whenever's twist, chosen by the owner over the roll; seed key 49a458f5. Signature move: the sentence with slots in Create, and the red fired tab.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
